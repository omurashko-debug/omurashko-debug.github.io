Privacy Policy

Oleg rclone is a personal application used only by its author to access and manage the author's own Google Drive data through rclone.

Google user data

The application accesses Google Drive data only after the author explicitly authorizes access through Google OAuth.

Use of data

Google Drive data is used only to perform file operations requested by the author through rclone, such as listing, downloading, uploading, copying, moving, synchronizing, and deleting files.

Storage of data

This application does not operate an external server and does not independently collect or store Google user data. Files may be stored locally on devices controlled by the author when the author uses rclone to download, copy, or synchronize them.

Sharing of data

Google user data is not sold, shared, or transferred to third parties by this application.

Data retention and deletion

The application itself does not retain Google user data. Locally copied files remain under the author's control and can be deleted by the author at any time. OAuth access can be revoked through the author's Google Account security settings.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the application owner through the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
